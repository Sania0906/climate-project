### Slide 2: The Problem
**Title:** Agriculture is Running Dry
**Statistic:** 70% of global freshwater withdrawals are used for agriculture—much of it wasted through over-irrigation. (Source: World Bank)
**Text:** Farmers irrigate based on routine and guesswork. Without localized climate and soil data, they apply too much water, leading to depleted aquifers and high pumping emissions.

### Slide 3: Why Now?
**Title:** Climate Volatility Requires Precision
**Bullets:**
- Rising temperatures increase crop water demand unpredictably.
- Erratic rainfall makes traditional irrigation schedules obsolete.
- Groundwater levels are dropping at alarming rates.
**Visual:** Graph showing rising agricultural water demand vs dropping water tables.

### Slide 4: Our Solution
**Title:** Introducing AquaSync AI
**Text:** A predictive AI platform that tells farmers *exactly* how much water their crops need today, based on real-time weather and soil data.
**Visual:** Mockup of the Streamlit dashboard on a tablet.

### Slide 5: How It Works
**Title:** The AquaSync Architecture
**Visual:** Use the Mermaid architecture diagram (Data -> Random Forest ML -> Recommendation -> Impact).
