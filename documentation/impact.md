# Environmental and Economic Impact Model

## Impact Calculation Methodology

AquaSync AI calculates its impact using the following baseline assumptions:
- **Baseline Water Use:** Traditional flood/routine irrigation often applies 40% more water than required by the crop evapotranspiration (ETc) rate.
- **Energy Conversion:** Pumping 1,000 liters of groundwater typically requires ~0.8 kWh of electricity (depending on pump efficiency and water depth).
- **Emissions:** In fossil-heavy grids, 1 kWh equals roughly 0.5 kg to 0.8 kg of CO2 emissions.

### 1. Environmental Impact
- **Water Saved:** By limiting irrigation to the exact ML-predicted requirement, farmers save thousands of liters per hectare per cycle. 
  *(Calculation: Traditional applied water - ML Recommended Water = Water Saved)*
- **Reduced Emissions:** Less pumping means less electricity used.
  *(Calculation: Water Saved in Liters / 1000 * 0.8 kWh * 0.5 kg CO2 = CO2 Prevented)*

### 2. Social Impact
- **Decision Support:** Reduces the cognitive load on farmers.
- **Drought Resilience:** Early warning systems for water stress allow farmers to procure water or apply protective measures before permanent wilting point is reached.

### 3. Economic Impact
- **Cost Reduction:** Lower electricity/diesel bills for running water pumps.
- **Yield Protection:** Preventing over-irrigation reduces soil nutrient leaching and root rot, maintaining optimal yields.
