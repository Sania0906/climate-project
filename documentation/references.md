# References and Sources

When defending this project, cite these statistics (all are based on real, verifiable global data):

1. **Agricultural Water Use:** "Agriculture accounts for 70% of global freshwater withdrawals." 
   *Source: World Bank (Water in Agriculture Report)*
2. **Irrigation Inefficiency:** "Surface irrigation efficiency is often only 30-40%, meaning more than half the water applied is wasted through evaporation or deep percolation."
   *Source: Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO)*
3. **Climate Change & Agriculture:** "Climate change is increasing the frequency of droughts, making water availability highly unpredictable for farmers."
   *Source: IPCC Sixth Assessment Report*
4. **Energy Water Nexus:** "Pumping groundwater for irrigation accounts for a massive percentage of agricultural energy use, contributing significantly to greenhouse gas emissions."
   *Source: UN Water / Ministry of Power (India)*

*Note for Judges:* The dataset used in the prototype is synthetically generated using standard agronomic principles (crop coefficients, base evapotranspiration) to demonstrate the machine learning architecture.
